Description

Walnut

  • Name: Walnut 
  • Botanical Name: Juglans regia 
  • Family Name: Juglandaceae 
  • Commercial Part: Kernel (nut) 

Product Description

Walnuts are nutrient-rich nuts enclosed in a hard brown shell with kernels shaped like butterfly lobes. Revered both for their ornamental trees and edible kernels, walnuts are harvested in winter but available year-round. They are widely used in snacks, bakery items, confectionery, and gourmet cooking. Their mild, earthy taste and crunchy texture make them a global favorite.

Applications

  • Consumed as a healthy snack, raw or roasted 
  • Widely used in bakery products, chocolates, and confectionery 
  • Added to salads, breakfast cereals, and dairy preparations 
  • Used in sauces, desserts, and festive cuisines 
  • Cold-pressed walnut oil used in cooking and cosmetics 

Nutritional & Health Benefits

  • Rich source of omega-3 fatty acids for heart and brain health 
  • Packed with protein, fiber, magnesium, and antioxidants 
  • Supports bone strength and reduces inflammation 
  • Promotes improved memory and cognitive function 
  • Helps regulate cholesterol and blood pressure
