About Us

Delivering Premium Food Products Worldwide.

D2G Foodstuff Trading L.L.C is a Dubai-licensed trading company specializing in spices, grains, pulses, dairy, fresh & frozen meat, eggs, flour, ghee & vegetable oils, and fresh & dried fruits and vegetables. We source quality products from around the world and supply them to hotels, restaurants, hypermarkets, supermarkets, and distributors.

Our mission is to ensure that every client receives quality food products, timely delivery, and competitive pricing — backed by full legal compliance under our Dubai Department of Economy & Tourism trade license.

  • Extensive sourcing from trusted global farms.
  • Hygienic processing and modern packaging.
  • Consistent supply for bulk and retail clients.
  • Strong distribution across UAE and international markets.
